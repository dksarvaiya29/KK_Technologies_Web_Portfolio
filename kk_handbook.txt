6. Logo, Typography & Layout Rules

Logo integrity

q Use only the approved original KK Technologies logo asset supplied by the owner.
q Never redraw, regenerate, distort, recolor, crop, stretch, or alter logo lettering or symbol proportions.
q Maintain clear space around the logo and ensure strong contrast against the background.
q Do not place effects over the logo that reduce legibility.
q If a transparent or vector logo is unavailable, request the original asset rather than approximating it.

Typography direction

Role                     Preferred direction                    Usage
Display / hero headline  Montserrat ExtraBold or Poppins Bold   Short, strong headlines and campaign statements.

Body copy                Poppins Regular or a clean sans-serif  Readable supporting copy, descriptions, and captions.

Technical / code labels  JetBrains Mono                         Code snippets, interface labels, technical annotations.

Layout principles

q Use a clear hierarchy: one dominant headline, one visual focal point, concise support copy.
q Use generous negative space and consistent alignment.
q Use orange selectively for emphasis, CTA cues, and focal details.
q Keep body text readable on mobile; do not shrink text to force excess copy into a graphic.
q Use consistent spacing, grid alignment, corner radius, and icon style within a campaign.
q Avoid mixing unrelated visual metaphors in one composition.

Recommended design QA

q Logo unchanged and legible.
q Palette matches the selected variant.
q Headline is readable at phone size.
q Single clear focal point.
q No spelling or factual errors.
q CTA is specific and visible.
q Export dimensions and crop are correct.

8. Website & E-commerce Delivery Standards

Website delivery principles

q Start with business goals, audience, sitemap, and conversion actions.
q Design mobile-first and test real responsive breakpoints.
q Use clear navigation, legible typography, accessible contrast, and predictable interaction patterns.
q Optimize images and assets; avoid unnecessary scripts and plugins.
q Validate forms, links, menus, search, checkout, and error states.
q Provide ownership-aware handover: domain, hosting, CMS, credentials, backups, and renewal responsibilities.

Recommended website project stages

1 Discovery: business model, audience, goals, competitors, constraints.
2 Scope: pages, templates, integrations, content responsibilities, exclusions, acceptance criteria.
3 Information architecture: sitemap, user journeys, conversion paths.
4 UI/UX: wireframes, visual direction, responsive components, design approval.
5 Build: development, CMS configuration, integrations, content population.
6 QA: device/browser testing, forms, performance, SEO basics, security checks.
7 Launch: backup, DNS/hosting checks, analytics, redirects, final approval.
8 Handover: walkthrough, documentation, access transfer, maintenance options.

WooCommerce-specific checklist

q Product taxonomy, categories, attributes, variations, SKU and inventory rules.
q Product detail page template consistency and clear purchase CTA.
q Cart and checkout behavior, payment gateway, shipping zones, taxes and order emails.
q Mobile product gallery, pricing, quantity controls and sticky CTA behavior where appropriate.
q Test guest checkout, failed payment, out-of-stock, coupon and order confirmation paths.
q Confirm privacy policy, terms, refund/return and shipping information with the client.

Known project implementation context

single-product detail templates, shop/all-products, checkout, mobile responsiveness, and a dynamic product-page
PHP component. A recovery incident was resolved by disabling a problematic PHP file; a replacement file was later

11. Sales, Outreach & Follow-up SOP

Sales objective

Build a repeatable process that identifies businesses with a real digital problem, communicates the value of a
solution clearly, follows up consistently, and closes projects with written scope and expectations.

Lead generation workflow

1 Choose one niche or business segment for the outreach batch.
2 Build a prospect list using publicly available business information and legitimate channels.
3 Review each prospect's website, Google/business profile, social media, mobile experience, and enquiry path.
4 Record one or two specific, observable opportunities--not generic criticism.
5 Prioritize leads by fit, visible need, ability to contact a decision maker, and urgency.
6 Personalize outreach around the business outcome, not a long list of services.

Lead tracker fields

Field                             Purpose
Business name / niche / location  Identify the prospect and segment.

Website / social links            Reference for audit and personalization.

Observed problem                  Specific evidence of an opportunity.

Recommended improvement           Practical solution hypothesis.

Contact person / channel          Authorized point of contact and channel.

Stage                             New, researched, contacted, replied, discovery, proposal, won, lost, nurture.

Last contact / next follow-up     Prevent missed follow-ups.

Estimated value / probability     Internal forecast only; not a client promise.

Notes / objections                Preserve context and next action.

Suggested outreach message structure

1 Personal opening that proves the message is not mass spam.
2 One concrete observation about the current customer journey.
3 A concise business impact hypothesis.
4 A low-friction offer: short audit, idea, or 10-minute discussion.
5 A clear, respectful question.

Example outreach (adapt, do not send blindly)

   Hi [Name], I came across [Business] and noticed [specific observation]. There may be an opportunity
   to make it easier for customers to [desired action] through a clearer mobile experience / enquiry flow.

17. Security, Privacy & Responsible AI

Minimum security practices

q Use unique passwords and a password manager; enable multi-factor authentication.
q Grant least-privilege access and remove access when no longer needed.
q Never request or store OTPs, payment PINs, or personal passwords in chat or project notes.
q Use secure credential-sharing methods and rotate exposed keys immediately.
q Keep backups before production changes and document rollback steps.
q Do not upload confidential client information to external AI services without permission and policy review.

Responsible AI usage

q Treat AI outputs as drafts; verify code, facts, claims, and citations before delivery.
q Do not fabricate testimonials, performance metrics, certifications, case-study results, or product claims.
q Use client-approved assets and check image/font/music licensing and platform terms.
q Label synthetic product imagery where needed and replace it with authentic product photography when

     available.
q Keep human approval for legal, financial, health, identity, and other consequential content.
q Do not use AI to impersonate people or create deceptive customer communications.

Client data handling

Define what data is collected, why it is needed, who can access it, where it is stored, how long it is retained, and
how it is deleted or returned. Confirm applicable privacy and data-protection requirements for each market.
